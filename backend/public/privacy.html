HIREON — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

HIREON ("we", "our", "the app") is a parcel pickup-and-delivery service connecting customers who need something delivered with riders who deliver it. This policy explains what information we collect through the HIREON mobile app, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

DataCollected fromWhy
Name, phone numberYou, at signupAccount creation, login (OTP verification), order communication
Role & vehicle details (riders)You, at signupAssigning delivery orders to riders
Precise location (GPS)Your device, during an active deliveryLive order tracking, matching nearby riders, calculating routes and ETA
Pickup / delivery addressesYou, when booking an orderFulfilling the delivery
Payment detailsRazorpay (our payment processor)Processing UPI / card / netbanking payments — HIREON does not see or store your card or UPI PIN

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account (phone OTP login via Firebase)
  • To connect customers with nearby available riders
  • To show live delivery tracking and route/ETA to the customer
  • To process payments and pay out rider earnings
  • To send order status notifications
  • To provide customer support when you contact us

3. Location data

The rider app shares live GPS location with the backend only while a delivery is in progress, so the customer can track their order in real time. Location sharing stops once the delivery is completed or the rider goes offline.

4. Third-party services we use

  • Firebase (Google) — phone number authentication and push notifications
  • Razorpay — payment processing (UPI, cards, netbanking). Razorpay has its own privacy policy
  • Google Maps / Routes — map display and route/ETA calculation
  • MongoDB Atlas — secure database storage of app data

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

5. Data retention

We retain account and order data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution requirements after account closure.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to any records we are legally required to keep (e.g. completed payment records).

7. Data security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access-controlled databases, secure authentication) to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

8. Children's privacy

HIREON is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
